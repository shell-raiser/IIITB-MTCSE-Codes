Dota2 Senate
https://leetcode.com/problems/dota2-senate/description/
============

In the world of Dota2, there are two parties: the Radiant and the Dire.

The Dota2 senate consists of senators, each belonging to one of the two
parties. Each senator can ban one senator from the opposing party.

The voting process is round-based:

1. Senators take turns in the order they appear in the string (left to right).
2. On a senator's turn, they may ban one opposing senator's voting rights.
3. A banned senator loses their turn for the rest of the process.
4. This continues until only one party has voting senators left.

Return "Radiant" if the Radiant party wins, or "Dire" if the Dire party wins.

Optimal strategy: each senator bans the NEXT opposing senator who would
otherwise vote after them.

Input:
  A single string senate consisting only of 'R' and 'D'.

Output:
  A single string — "Radiant" or "Dire".

Constraints:
  1 <= senate.length <= 10^4
  senate[i] is either 'R' or 'D'

Example 1:
  Input:  RD
  Output: Radiant

Example 2:
  Input:  RDD
  Output: Dire

Example 3:
  Input:  RRDDD
  Output: Radiant

Example 4:
  Input:  DRDRR
  Output: Dire

Example 5:
  Input:  R
  Output: Radiant

Example 6:
  Input:  D
  Output: Dire
